📋 Terms of Use

Job Fit Analyzer · Effective from first use · Version 1.0

1. Authorised Use

This tool is provided exclusively for use by authorised HR, Talent Acquisition, and Recruitment professionals within the deploying organisation. Unauthorised access or use for purposes other than evaluating candidate-to-role fit is strictly prohibited.

2. Advisory Scores Only

All scores generated by this tool are advisory only. No hiring, rejection, shortlisting, or screening decision may be made solely on the basis of an AI-generated score. A qualified human reviewer must evaluate all results before taking any employment action.

3. Anti-Bias & Non-Discrimination

  • You must not use this tool to discriminate against candidates based on any protected characteristic (race, sex, age, disability, religion, national origin, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law).
  • You must review and remediate any bias warnings flagged by the JD bias scanner before using a score in any screening decision.
  • You must conduct periodic disparate impact testing as required by applicable employment AI regulations.

4. Regulatory Compliance

You are responsible for ensuring your organisation's use of this tool complies with all applicable employment AI regulations in your jurisdiction, including but not limited to: EU AI Act (Annex III), EEOC/Title VII, NYC Local Law 144, GDPR Art. 22, and the UK Equality Act 2010.

5. Data Handling

  • Resume and job profile text is processed in-browser only and is never transmitted to any external server.
  • You must not input data belonging to real candidates without their knowledge and consent.
  • Audit log data must be handled in accordance with your organisation's data retention and privacy policies.

6. Human-in-the-Loop Obligation

You acknowledge that the Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) advisory displayed after each analysis is a mandatory governance control. Scores in the 45–65% range must be escalated to a human reviewer and must not be used as the sole basis for any screening decision.

7. Limitation of Liability

This tool is provided as-is. The tool developer (Vishveshwar Rao) accepts no liability for employment decisions made using this tool. The deploying organisation assumes full legal responsibility for compliance with applicable employment, privacy, and AI regulations.

⚖️ AI Screening Disclosure

This tool uses an AI-powered keyword matching algorithm to assess resume-to-job-profile fit. Before proceeding, please acknowledge the following:

  • This score is advisory only — a human reviewer must make any final screening decision.
  • The analysis operates on skills and experience only. Demographic attributes (name, age, gender, address) are not scored.
  • Personal identifiers are stripped from the resume before analysis (de-proxying).
  • Results are logged for audit purposes in accordance with applicable employment AI regulations.
  • Candidates have the right to request a human review of any AI-assisted assessment.
